How to Approach the Interview

  • Read the question carefully before coding.
  • Explain your approach and why you chose it.
  • Consider edge cases and test your solution.
  • Discuss time and space complexity.
  • Communicate clearly and ask questions if needed.

How Are Scores Calculated?

Your performance score is based on six criteria. Each criterion contributes a fixed percentage to your final score.

Problem Understanding 20%

Understanding the requirements, constraints, and expected output.

Problem-Solving Approach 25%

Choosing a suitable algorithm and explaining the reasoning behind your approach.

Correctness & Edge Cases 25%

Producing a correct solution and considering boundary conditions and unusual inputs.

Code Quality 15%

Writing readable, organised code with clear variable names and logical structure.

Complexity Analysis 10%

Correctly explaining time and space complexity.

Communication 5%

Explaining your thinking clearly and responding constructively to feedback.

Total Score 100%
